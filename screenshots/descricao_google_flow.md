# Spirit Summoners TD — descrição pro Google Flow

## Resumo (PT)
Spirit Summoners TD é um tower defense no Roblox onde as torres são espíritos elementais chibi invocados por gacha, no estilo Summoners War. O jogador começa num santuário em uma ilha flutuante no céu (grama, cerejeiras rosas, nuvens fofas, pôr do sol dourado), invoca espíritos no altar de cristal, monta um time de 4 e entra num dos 5 portais coloridos. A run acontece num santuário elemental em ruínas: espíritos corrompidos seguem um caminho único em 50 waves, com chefes nas waves 10/20/30/40 e um chefe final de 3 fases na 50. Fora da run, o jogador sobe nível, evolui, desperta e equipa runas nos espíritos.

- 9 famílias de espíritos: Dragãozinho, Kitsune, Fênix, Golem, Pixie (fada), Coruja, Sapo, Tanuki, Grifo.
- 5 elementos e cores: Fogo `#FF6A3D`, Água `#3DA5FF`, Vento `#4CD98A`, Luz `#FFD84A`, Trevas `#8A4DFF`.
- Visual: toon, cores vibrantes, cabeças grandes, silhuetas legíveis, UI marrom/dourada estilo Summoners War.

## Prompt base (EN — cole no Flow junto com as prints)
Cute chibi fantasy tower defense game in Roblox style, "Spirit Summoners TD". A floating sky-island sanctuary with bright green grass, pink cherry blossom trees, fluffy white clouds and a warm golden sunset sky. In the center, a summoning altar of cream stone and gold with a glowing purple crystal. Five ornate white-and-gold portal arches glowing orange, blue, green, yellow and purple. Adorable chibi elemental spirit creatures — baby dragons, kitsune foxes, phoenixes, stone golems, fairies, owls, frogs, tanukis and griffins — each in fire, water, wind, light or dark colors. Vibrant toon shading, soft lighting, playful and magical mood, high detail, game key art.

## Prompts por cena
1. **Capa / thumbnail:** Epic key art of five chibi elemental spirits (fire baby dragon, water fairy, wind kitsune, light griffin, dark phoenix) posing heroically in front of glowing portals on a floating sky island at sunset, title space at the top, vibrant toon style, Roblox game thumbnail, 16:9.
2. **Invocação:** A glowing summoning altar on a floating island releasing a beam of rainbow light as a legendary 5-star chibi spirit appears, sparkles and magic runes, golden sunset, cute toon style.
3. **Batalha:** Top-down tower defense battle in ruined elemental temple ruins, chibi spirit towers shooting fire, water and lightning beams at a line of corrupted shadow spirits walking along a stone path toward a glowing base crystal, colorful VFX, toon style.
4. **Chefe:** Giant corrupted elemental boss spirit with a dark purple aura under a blood-red moon, tiny cute chibi spirit towers fighting back, dramatic lighting, toon style.
5. **Roster:** Lineup of 25 cute chibi elemental spirits in 5 rows by element (fire orange, water blue, wind green, light gold, dark purple), clean background, character sheet, toon style.
